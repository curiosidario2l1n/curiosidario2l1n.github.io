Privacy Policy: Curiosidário Postagem

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Curiosidário Postagem (the Service) is a private, internal automation tool operated by Rodrigo Kasuaki, an individual based in Brazil. It is used exclusively to publish original short educational videos created by the operator to the operator's own accounts of the Curiosidário channel on YouTube (@curiosidario2l1n), Instagram (@curiosidario2l1n) and TikTok. The Service has no external users and is not offered to the public.

Platforms and terms

The Service uses the YouTube API Services, the Instagram API (Meta) and the TikTok Content Posting API and Login Kit. Use of the Service is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, the Instagram Terms of Use, the Meta Privacy Policy, the TikTok Terms of Service and the TikTok Privacy Policy.

Information we access

Only with the account owner's authorization (OAuth), the Service:

  • reads basic profile information of the operator's own accounts (account ID, username, display name);
  • uploads and publishes videos created by the operator to the operator's own accounts;
  • reads public statistics of the operator's own videos (views, likes, comment counts);
  • reads public comments on the operator's own videos and publishes replies on behalf of the operator.

The Service does not access private data of any other account, does not access direct messages, and does not collect personal information from viewers beyond what the platforms make publicly visible on the operator's own videos.

How we use it

Information is used only to publish the operator's content, to reply to comments on the operator's own videos and to produce aggregated internal insights for the operator. It is never sold, shared for advertising, or used to identify, profile or contact individual viewers.

Third-party processing

Video scripts and comment text may be processed by the Google Gemini API to draft scripts and replies. Video scripts (never viewer data) are sent to JSON2Video to render videos. Notifications are sent to the operator's own Telegram account.

Storage and retention

Data is processed on the operator's own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted in the operator's private automation tool. Raw comments and statistics are kept only in execution logs, deleted automatically within 30 days, except a short log of replied comment IDs used to avoid duplicate replies.

Revoking access and data deletion

The account owner can revoke the Service's access at any time in the security settings of Google, Instagram/Meta or TikTok. Anyone who wants their comment or related data removed can delete the comment on the platform or email kasuaki123@gmail.com, and it will be deleted within 30 days.
